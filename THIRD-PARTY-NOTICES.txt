FFmpeg (video and audio processing)                                   GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clipper ships an unmodified LGPL build of FFmpeg as a separate program (ffmpeg.exe / ffprobe in the engine folder).
- FFmpeg source code: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg (the release branch named in "ffmpeg -version")
- Build scripts used (LGPL configuration, no GPL parts): https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
- License text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
You may replace ffmpeg.exe with your own build of FFmpeg. The Python packages PyAV and OpenCV (below) also include
LGPL FFmpeg libraries, with the same source and license.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Claude AI (optional)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With your own Anthropic API key, Clipper sends the spoken words of your video as text (and a few still frames for
action videos) to Anthropic's Claude to pick moments and check captions. Use of Claude is governed by Anthropic's
terms: https://www.anthropic.com/legal. Without a key, nothing is sent anywhere.
